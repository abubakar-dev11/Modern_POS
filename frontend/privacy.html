Privacy Policy

How Modern POS may handle information when you use the application.

Effective date: 29 October 2026

1. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, Modern POS may process account details such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and branch details. Users may also enter business information such as products, stock records, sales, customer details, and payment records.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide and operate POS features, authenticate users, associate records with the relevant business, support customer service, protect the service, and troubleshoot errors. Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or notice.

3. Access and sharing

Information may be visible to authorized users within the relevant business according to their account roles and permissions. Information may also be processed by hosting, database, security, or other service providers needed to run the application. We do not intend to sell personal information. Do not assume that information is shared only with the people you expect; configure user permissions carefully.

4. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Use strong passwords, restrict staff permissions, and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

5. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while needed to operate the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, or protect security. The exact retention and deletion process depends on the application’s current technical setup and applicable law. Contact us to ask about a particular record.

6. Cookies and local storage

The web application may use browser storage, such as local storage, to keep session or authentication information so you can remain signed in. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to affect or access that session. Sign out on shared devices and keep your device secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You may contact us to ask about these requests. We may need to verify your identity and may retain some records where legally permitted or required.

8. Children

Modern POS is intended for business use and is not designed for children to create accounts independently.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application changes. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page.
